How it works

A stranger's eye on your forms.

The scanner visits the page from our server, exactly as a member of the public would. It does not log in, submit forms or guess passwords. It reads what is already public and compares it with the habits Act 843 expects.

  1. 01

    Find the doors

    Every form and field is read. Fields that collect names, phone and MoMo numbers, Ghana Card, date of birth, addresses or payment details are flagged.

  2. 02

    Check the locks

    HTTPS, where forms send their data, browser security headers, tracking scripts, a privacy notice, consent boxes and who is responsible.

  3. 03

    Explain in plain words

    Each finding is tied to one of the eight principles in section 17, with what it means for real people and a fix you can hand to your web designer.

The yardstick

Eight principles, one woven cloth.

Section 17 of the Act sets out eight principles. Every check the scanner runs is filed under one of them.
